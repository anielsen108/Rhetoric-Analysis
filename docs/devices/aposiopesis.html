Omission & Flow

Aposiopesis

ap-oh-sy-oh-PEE-sis / ˌæpəsaɪəˈpiːsɪs

Breaking off mid-sentence and leaving it unfinished — the dash into silence. The unsaid completion is louder than words could be: threat, grief, or the unspeakable, delegated to the reader's imagination. Grammar fails on purpose, and the failure is the meaning.

"I will have such revenges on you both, / That all the world shall— I will do such things—" — King Lear

Don't confuse it with Ellipsis. Ellipsis omits words the reader can reconstruct; aposiopesis abandons the sentence at a cliff edge.
