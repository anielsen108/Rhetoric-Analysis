Sound & Diction

Assonance

ASS-uh-nunce / ˈæsənəns

Repetition of vowel sounds within nearby words — the deep green sea. Vowels carry a line's music and emotional temperature: long vowels slow and mourn, short vowels patter and quicken. Assonance is rhyme's shy cousin, binding lines below conscious notice.

"in Xanadu did Kubla Khan / A stately pleasure-dome decree" — the a's and u's

Don't confuse it with Alliteration. Alliteration repeats initial consonants; consonance repeats consonants anywhere; assonance is vowels only.
