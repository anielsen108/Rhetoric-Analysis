Irony & Understatement

Irony

EYE-ruh-nee / ˈaɪrəni

A gap between what is said and what is meant (verbal irony), between what a character knows and what the audience knows (dramatic irony), or between expectation and outcome (situational irony). The reader who catches the gap is flattered into complicity with the author — irony builds an in-group of those who get it.

"Brutus is an honourable man." — Antony, meaning the reverse

Don't confuse it with Paradox. Sarcasm is verbal irony weaponized at a target. Paradox asserts a real contradiction; irony merely means other than it says.
