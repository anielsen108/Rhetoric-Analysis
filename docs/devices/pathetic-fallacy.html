Pathetic Fallacy

puh-THET-ik FAL-uh-see

Nature mirroring human emotion — storms for turmoil, spring for hope — as if the world felt what the character feels. Ruskin coined it as a criticism (the 'fallacy' of emotional projection), but writers use it deliberately to make setting do the psychological narration.

The storm on the heath matching Lear's madness.

Don't confuse it with Personification. Ordinary personification needn't reflect anyone's inner state; pathetic fallacy specifically syncs outer weather to inner weather.
