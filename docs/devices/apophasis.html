Irony & Understatement

Apophasis

uh-POF-uh-sis / əˈpɒfəsɪs

Asserting something by declaring you will not mention it — 'I won't dwell on his record.' The denial performs innocence while the content lands anyway; the audience receives the accusation minus the accountability. A favorite of politicians and Mark Antony.

"I come to bury Caesar, not to praise him."

Don't confuse it with Irony. Paralipsis and praeteritio are near-synonyms; irony is the wider genus of saying-other-than-meaning.
