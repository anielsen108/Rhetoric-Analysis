Sound & Diction

Archaism

AR-kay-iz-um / ˈɑːrkeɪɪzəm

Deliberately old-fashioned language — four score, thou — used for its aura. Old words bring their era's authority with them: scripture, epic, law.

"Four score and seven years ago…" — Lincoln

Don't confuse it with Allusion. Allusion points to a specific prior text; archaism borrows a period's diction wholesale without citing anyone.
