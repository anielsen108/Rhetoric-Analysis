Invocation

in-voh-KAY-shun

A formal appeal to a muse, deity, or power to inspire and authorize the work — the epic's opening gesture. It stages humility (the poet claims to be a vessel) while performing supreme ambition, and signals to the reader what tradition the work is claiming descent from.

"Sing, Heav'nly Muse…" — Paradise Lost

Don't confuse it with Apostrophe. Apostrophe is any address to the absent; invocation specifically petitions for aid at a threshold.
