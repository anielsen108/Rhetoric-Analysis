Metaphor & Figuration

Personification

per-sah-nih-fih-KAY-shun / pərˌsɒnɪfɪˈkeɪʃən

Granting human qualities, actions, or feelings to non-human things — nations conceive, winds sigh, justice sleeps. It converts description into drama: once a thing can act and intend, it can be a character in the sentence, with motives the reader instinctively reads.

"Because I could not stop for Death – / He kindly stopped for me –" — Dickinson

Don't confuse it with Prosopopoeia, Pathetic Fallacy. Prosopopoeia goes further and gives the thing a speaking voice. Pathetic fallacy is personification of nature specifically, colored by the observer's emotion.
