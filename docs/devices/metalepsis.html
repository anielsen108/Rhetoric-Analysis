Metaphor & Figuration

Metalepsis

met-uh-LEP-sis / ˌmɛtəˈlɛpsɪs

A figure built on another figure — reference at a remove, where the reader must traverse a chain of associations to arrive. In narrative theory it is the breach between levels: a character addressing the author, a narrator stepping into the story. Either way, a transgression of the frame that exposes the frame.

"I have drunk milk from the cow that scattered the seed" — reaching an idea through chained figures

Don't confuse it with Metonymy. Simple metonymy makes one associative hop; metalepsis needs at least two. The narrative sense (frame-breaking) is the common modern usage.
