In Medias Res

in MAY-dee-ahs RACE / ɪn ˈmɛdiəs ˈreɪs

Beginning in the middle of the action, with context withheld and backstory deferred. The reader arrives late to a world already running and must orient by inference — which manufactures instant momentum and flatters the reader into detective work. Epic convention since Homer; the default for modern openings.

Paradise Lost opening after the fall of the rebel angels, not before.

Don't confuse it with Analepsis. Analepsis (flashback) is how the deferred backstory later arrives; in medias res is the choice of entry point itself.
