Repetition & Refrain

Diacope

dy-ACK-oh-pee / daɪˈækəpiː

Repetition of a word or phrase with a short interruption between the repeats — the pattern of "Bond. James Bond." The intervening material gets framed by the echo, and the return of the word feels like insistence: the speaker circles back because once was not enough.

"Put out the light, and then put out the light." — Othello

Don't confuse it with Anaphora, Epizeuxis. Epizeuxis repeats with no gap at all; anaphora repeats only at clause openings.
