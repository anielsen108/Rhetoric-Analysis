Oxymoron

ok-see-MOR-on / ˌɒksɪˈmɔːrɒn

Two contradictory words fused into one phrase — deafening silence, living death. The clash sits inside a single unit, so it can't be resolved by reading on; both terms must be held at once.

"Parting is such sweet sorrow." — Romeo and Juliet

Don't confuse it with Paradox. Paradox is a contradictory *statement* that unfolds; oxymoron is the two-word spark.
