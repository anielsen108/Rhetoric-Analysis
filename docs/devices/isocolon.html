Balance & Opposition

Isocolon

eye-soh-KOH-lon / ˌaɪsoʊˈkoʊlən

Successive phrases or clauses of matching length and identical grammatical shape — parallelism with the meter of a scale in balance. Because the units weigh the same, the sentence sounds pre-measured, judicial, inevitable; two-part isocolon suggests fairness, three-part suggests completeness.

"Veni, vidi, vici." — Julius Caesar

Don't confuse it with Parallelism. All isocolon is parallelism, but parallelism only requires matching structure, not matching *length and rhythm*.
