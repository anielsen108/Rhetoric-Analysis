Balance & Opposition

Isocolon

eye-soh-KOH-lon / ˌaɪsoʊˈkoʊlən

Phrases or clauses in a row with the same shape and nearly the same length. The equal weights make the sentence sound measured and deliberate: two parts suggest fairness, three suggest completeness.

"Veni, vidi, vici." — Julius Caesar

Don't confuse it with Parallelism. All isocolon is parallelism, but parallelism only requires matching structure, not matching *length and rhythm*.
