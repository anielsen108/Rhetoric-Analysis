Sound & Diction

Tmesis

TMEE-sis / təˈmiːsɪs

Splitting a word or fixed phrase and wedging another word inside — abso-bloody-lutely. The intruder detonates inside the host word, which makes the emphasis feel involuntary, as if feeling burst the word open from within.

"un-bloody-believable"

Don't confuse it with Parenthesis. Infixation is the linguist's term for the same surgery; parenthesis interrupts a sentence, tmesis interrupts a single word.
