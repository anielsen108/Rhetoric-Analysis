Brachylogia

brak-ih-LOH-jee-uh / ˌbrækɪˈloʊdʒiə

Extreme compression: single words strung without conjunctions — a telegraphic staccato. Each word gets a full stress-beat, and the missing grammar reads as urgency, shell-shock, or command. Prolonged, it becomes a style of exhaustion where syntax has been rationed.

"We real cool. We / Left school." — Gwendolyn Brooks

Don't confuse it with Asyndeton. Asyndeton omits conjunctions between phrases or clauses; brachylogia does it between bare words.
