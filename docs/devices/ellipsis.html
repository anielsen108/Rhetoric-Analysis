Omission & Flow

Ellipsis

ee-LIP-sis / ɪˈlɪpsɪs

Leaving out words the grammar needs but the context supplies: 'I took the high road; she, the low.' The reader restores what's missing, and the gap itself draws attention.

"And he to England shall along with you." — Hamlet

Don't confuse it with Aposiopesis. Aposiopesis breaks off irrecoverably; elliptical omissions are recoverable from the surrounding grammar.
