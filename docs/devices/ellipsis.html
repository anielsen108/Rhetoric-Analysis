Omission & Flow

Ellipsis

ee-LIP-sis / ɪˈlɪpsɪs

Omitting words that grammar requires but context supplies — 'I took the high road; she, the low.' The reader silently restores what's missing, becoming co-author of the sentence. Ellipsis compresses, quickens, and flatters; what it deletes, it also emphasizes by the gap.

"And he to England shall along with you." — Hamlet

Don't confuse it with Aposiopesis. Aposiopesis breaks off irrecoverably; elliptical omissions are recoverable from the surrounding grammar.
