Repetition & Refrain

Polyptoton

pah-lip-TOH-ton / pəˈlɪptətɒn

Repeating a word in different grammatical forms — please/pleasure, judge/judgment, dying/death. The root recurs while its function shifts, so the idea seems to permeate the whole grammar of the sentence: the concept isn't just mentioned, it conjugates through reality.

"The Greeks are strong, and skilful to their strength, / Fierce to their skill, and to their fierceness valiant." — Shakespeare

Don't confuse it with Epizeuxis, Diacope. Simple repetition (epizeuxis, diacope) repeats the same form; polyptoton morphs the word as it returns.
