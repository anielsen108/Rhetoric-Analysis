Syntax & Word Order

Anastrophe

uh-NAS-truh-fee / əˈnæstrəfi

Inversion of the usual order of a pair of words — adjective after noun, preposition after object. A local, controlled reversal that archaizes or poeticizes: the reader hears older English, verse convention, or Yoda, depending on dosage.

"the forest primeval" — Longfellow

Don't confuse it with Hyperbaton. The gentle end of hyperbaton's spectrum; periodic structure delays whole clauses, not word pairs.
