Metaphor & Figuration

Catachresis

kat-uh-KREE-sis / ˌkætəˈkriːsɪs

A deliberately strained or 'wrong' metaphor — using a word where no literal word exists, or forcing a term outside its category until the semantic misfit itself is the effect. It reads as slightly broken on purpose; the wrongness defamiliarizes, making the reader re-see an ordinary thing as if newly named.

"the watery part of the world" — Melville, for the ocean

Don't confuse it with Metaphor. A dead metaphor ('table leg') no longer registers as figurative; catachresis keeps its torque — it must still feel misapplied.
