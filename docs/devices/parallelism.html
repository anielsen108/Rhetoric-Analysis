Parallelism

PAIR-uh-lel-iz-um / ˈpærəlɛlɪzəm

Expressing coordinate ideas in coordinate grammatical form — matching phrases with phrases, clauses with clauses. The repeated structure tells the reader these items belong to one set and invites comparison among them; break the pattern and the breaking item gets the spotlight.

"…conceived in Liberty, and dedicated to the proposition…" — Lincoln

Don't confuse it with Antithesis, Isocolon. Isocolon adds equal length and rhythm; antithesis is parallelism put in service of contrast.
