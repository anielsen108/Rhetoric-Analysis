Mythopoesis

mith-oh-poh-EE-sis

Myth-making: narration pitched at the register of origin stories, where characters become archetypes and events become cosmology. The prose signals that what happens here explains the world, not just the plot — genealogies, epithets, and ritual repetition are its instruments.

"Many years later… his father took him to discover ice." — García Márquez founding a world

Don't confuse it with Allusion. Allusion borrows an existing myth; mythopoesis manufactures the mythic register itself.
