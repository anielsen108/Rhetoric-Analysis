Syntax & Word Order

Enumeration / Accumulatio

ee-noo-mer-AH-tee-oh

Amassing details, examples, or charges in a deliberate pile — the catalogue as argument. Sheer quantity becomes quality: by the tenth item the reader stops evaluating and starts surrendering. Whitman's catalogues make abundance into democracy; a prosecutor's make it into guilt.

Whitman's catalogues of trades and bodies in "Song of Myself"

Don't confuse it with. Simple listing is neutral; enumeratio is a list with rhetorical intent — the pile *is* the point. Congeries is the near-synonym.
