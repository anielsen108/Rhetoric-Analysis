Metaphor & Figuration

Allusion

uh-LOO-zhun / əˈluːʒən

An unflagged reference to another text, event, or figure that imports its whole context into the new sentence. The device runs on shared knowledge: readers who catch it receive a second channel of meaning, and the borrowed authority (biblical, classical, literary) arrives without the cost of citation.

"all men are created equal" inside the Gettysburg Address — importing the Declaration.

Don't confuse it with Metalepsis. Quotation announces its borrowing; allusion smuggles it. Metalepsis is allusion at a further remove, referencing through a chain.
