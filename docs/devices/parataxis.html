Parataxis

pair-uh-TAK-sis / ˌpærəˈtæksɪs

Placing clauses side by side without subordination — no because, although, or which to rank one thought under another. Everything sits on one flat logical plane, so the reader must supply the causal connections the grammar withholds. The signature syntax of Hemingway, the King James Bible, and hard-boiled narration.

"The sun rose. He walked to the window. The street was empty."

Don't confuse it with Asyndeton. Hypotaxis is its opposite: clauses nested by subordination. Asyndeton concerns missing conjunctions; parataxis concerns missing *hierarchy*.
