Irony & Understatement

Euphemism

YOO-fuh-miz-um / ˈjuːfəmɪzəm

Substituting a mild or indirect expression for a harsh or taboo one — passed away, let go, collateral damage. Every euphemism is a small argument about what should not be looked at directly; writers deploy them straight (tact), or expose them ironically to indict the evasion.

"He is helping the police with their inquiries."

Don't confuse it with Periphrasis. Periphrasis is roundabout wording for any reason; euphemism specifically launders the unpleasant.
