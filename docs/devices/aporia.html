Aporia

uh-POR-ee-uh / əˈpɔːriə

Performed doubt or perplexity — the speaker pauses to wonder aloud what to say, how to proceed, whether the thing can be said at all. The hesitation dramatizes honesty and difficulty; sometimes it is real (the essay thinking), sometimes staged (the lawyer 'at a loss' before a jury).

"To be, or not to be, that is the question" — deliberation as spectacle

Don't confuse it with Rhetorical Question. A rhetorical question presumes its answer; aporia performs not having one.
