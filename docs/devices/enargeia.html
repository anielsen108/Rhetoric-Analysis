Enargeia / Vivid Specificity

en-AR-gay-uh / ɛnɑːrˈɡeɪə

Description so concrete and sensory that the audience seems to witness rather than read — the classical virtue of 'bringing before the eyes.' It is precision recruited as persuasion: the detail's specificity vouches for the whole account's truth.

Homer lingering on the boar's-tusk helmet, strap by strap.

Don't confuse it with. Ekphrasis is enargeia aimed at an artwork; imagery is the broad modern term without the persuasive charge.
