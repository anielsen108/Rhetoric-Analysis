Parenthesis

puh-REN-thuh-sis / pəˈrɛnθəsɪs

An aside inserted mid-sentence — set off by dashes, brackets, or commas — that interrupts the syntax without joining it. The interruption stages two time-streams at once: the sentence's argument and the mind commenting on it live. It is the printed trace of a speaking voice that can't help itself.

"—never mind how long precisely—" — Melville

Don't confuse it with. An appositive renames the noun beside it; a parenthesis digresses without renaming anything.
