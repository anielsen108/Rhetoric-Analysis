Irony & Understatement

Periphrasis

puh-RIF-ruh-sis / pəˈrɪfrəsɪs

Saying something in deliberately more words than needed — a roundabout route in place of the direct name. The detour can elevate (epic circumlocution), evade, or characterize a speaker who cannot bring themselves to say the plain thing; the reader always registers the swerve around the missing word.

"the finny tribe" for fish, in Augustan verse

Don't confuse it with Euphemism, Pleonasm. Euphemism detours specifically around the offensive; pleonasm adds redundant words without rerouting the meaning.
