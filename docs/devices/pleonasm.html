Pleonasm

PLEE-oh-naz-um / ˈpliːənæzəm

Redundancy of words — using more than the grammar strictly needs: I saw it with my own eyes. Handled deliberately, the surplus is emphasis or ritual fullness (KJV: 'answered and said'); handled carelessly, it is padding. The effect depends on the reader sensing the extra weight as chosen.

"free gift", "burnt fire" — or ritual doubling in scripture

Don't confuse it with Periphrasis. Tautology repeats the same *idea*; pleonasm repeats within one construction. Periphrasis reroutes; pleonasm just doubles.
