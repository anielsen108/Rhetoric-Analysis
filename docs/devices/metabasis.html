Metabasis

meh-TAB-uh-sis / mɛˈtæbəsɪs

A brief transitional summary: what has been said, what comes next. It is the argument turning to face the audience and showing its map — an act of confidence that also disciplines the reader's memory into the writer's preferred shape.

"So much for the causes; now for the consequences."

Don't confuse it with. Metanoia revises a statement; metabasis merely pivots between sections.
