Antimetabole

an-tee-muh-TAB-oh-lee / ˌæntimɛˈtæbəliː

Repeating the same words in reverse order — A-B / B-A with the identical vocabulary. The perfect mirror suggests the speaker has examined the idea from both directions and found the reversal more true; it is chiasmus at its most audible and most quotable.

"When the going gets tough, the tough get going."

Don't confuse it with Chiasmus. Every antimetabole is a chiasmus, but chiasmus can invert structure or sense without repeating the exact words.
