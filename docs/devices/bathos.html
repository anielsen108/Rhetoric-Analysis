Bathos

BAY-thos / ˈbeɪθɒs

A sudden fall from the elevated to the trivial — the sublime stubbing its toe. When accidental it is failed grandeur; when engineered, it is deflationary comedy that punctures pretension by letting the reader feel the drop in altitude bodily.

"Here thou, great Anna! whom three realms obey, / Dost sometimes counsel take — and sometimes tea." — Pope

Don't confuse it with. Pathos moves; bathos drops. Anticlimax is the structural name for the same descent.
