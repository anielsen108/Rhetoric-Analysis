Irony & Understatement

Hyperbole

hy-PER-buh-lee / haɪˈpɜːrbəliː

Exaggeration beyond literal credibility, offered in the confidence that the reader will scale it back — and land exactly where the writer wanted. The inflation isn't meant to deceive but to transmit intensity: the size of the overstatement measures the size of the feeling.

"I had to wait in the station for ten days — an eternity." — Conrad

Don't confuse it with Litotes, Bathos. Its opposite pole is understatement/litotes. When exaggeration collapses into the trivial for comic effect, that's bathos.
