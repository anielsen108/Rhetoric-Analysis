Amplificatio

am-plih-fih-KAH-tee-oh

Expanding and intensifying a point through repetition, accumulation, and escalation — returning to the same idea with greater force each pass. Where enumeration adds instances, amplification adds magnitude: the theme is not just restated but enlarged until it fills the reader's whole field of view.

Cicero circling Catiline's treachery in wave after rising wave.

Don't confuse it with. Enumeratio lists; amplificatio inflates. Auxesis is its close kin, arranging terms in ascending force.
