Narrative & Time

Free Indirect Discourse

(FID)

Third-person narration that takes on a character's voice and judgments without quotation marks or 'she thought' tags. The character's mind arrives inside the narrator's sentences, and irony lives in the gap between the two.

"What a lark! What a plunge!" — Mrs. Dalloway's mind in the narrator's sentence

Don't confuse it with. Direct discourse quotes; indirect discourse reports ('she thought that…'); FID deletes the tag and lets the voices blend.
