Sound & Diction

Neologism

nee-OL-uh-jiz-um / niˈɒlədʒɪzəm

A coined word — invented outright or fused from existing parts — filling a gap the lexicon didn't know it had. The reader parses it from its components and the surrounding pressure, and the small act of decoding creates ownership: you understand a word no dictionary taught you.

"'Twas brillig, and the slithy toves…" — Carroll

Don't confuse it with Catachresis. Catachresis misuses an existing word; neologism mints a new one. A portmanteau is a neologism made by fusion (slithy = slimy + lithe).
