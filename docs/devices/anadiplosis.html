Repetition & Refrain

Anadiplosis

an-uh-dih-PLOH-sis / ænədɪˈploʊsɪs

The last word of one clause becomes the first word of the next, linking clauses like a chain. Each link hands its weight to the next, so the sequence feels like consequence — one thing leading inexorably to another. Stacked anadiplosis ascending in importance becomes gradatio (climax).

"Fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. Hate leads to suffering." — Yoda

Don't confuse it with Epanalepsis. Gradatio is anadiplosis sustained up a ladder of intensity; epanalepsis circles back within a single clause.
