Rhetorical Question

(erotema: air-oh-TEE-muh)

A question posed for effect, whose answer is presumed or performed rather than sought. It conscripts the reader: you supply the answer, and having supplied it, you feel you argued it. A string of rhetorical questions simulates interrogation without surrendering control for a second.

"Hath not a Jew eyes?" — The Merchant of Venice

Don't confuse it with Aporia. Hypophora asks and then *answers its own* question; aporia performs genuine (or staged) doubt.
