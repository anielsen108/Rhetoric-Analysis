Rhetorical Question

(erotema: air-oh-TEE-muh)

A question asked for effect, with the answer assumed rather than sought. The reader supplies the answer — and, having supplied it, half believes it.

"Hath not a Jew eyes?" — The Merchant of Venice

Don't confuse it with Aporia. Hypophora asks and then *answers its own* question; aporia performs genuine (or staged) doubt.
