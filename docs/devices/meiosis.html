Irony & Understatement

Meiosis / Understatement

my-OH-sis / maɪˈoʊsɪs

Deliberately representing something as smaller or less significant than it is — calling a wound 'a scratch.' The gap between the thing and its label transfers the unstated magnitude to the reader's imagination, which reliably overshoots; understatement is how stoics boast.

"'Tis but a flesh wound."

Don't confuse it with Litotes. Litotes is understatement achieved specifically through negation ('not unkind'); meiosis just shrinks the label.
