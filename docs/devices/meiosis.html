Irony & Understatement

Meiosis / Understatement

my-OH-sis / maɪˈoʊsɪs

Deliberately calling something smaller than it is — a wound is 'a scratch.' The gap between the thing and its label makes the reader imagine the real size, which usually overshoots.

"'Tis but a flesh wound."

Don't confuse it with Litotes. Litotes is understatement achieved specifically through negation ('not unkind'); meiosis just shrinks the label.
