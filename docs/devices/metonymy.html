Metonymy

meh-TAH-nuh-mee / mɛˈtɒnəmi

Substituting a thing's name with something associated with it — the crown for the monarchy, the White House for the administration, the sword for war. The figure works by contiguity (association) rather than resemblance, and it quietly encodes a worldview: what you choose as the stand-in reveals what you take to be the essence.

"The pen is mightier than the sword."

Don't confuse it with Metaphor, Synecdoche. Metaphor maps by resemblance; metonymy by association. Synecdoche is the special case where the substitute is a *part* of the whole.
