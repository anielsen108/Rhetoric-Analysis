Omission & Flow

Asyndeton

uh-SIN-duh-ton / əˈsɪndətɒn

Omitting the conjunctions a list or clause-series would normally carry ("I came, I saw, I conquered" rather than "and I conquered"). The missing connectives compress and accelerate: items arrive as raw succession, implying the list could go on forever or that there is no time for grammar.

"…and that government of the people, by the people, for the people, shall not perish." — Lincoln (phrases without 'and')

Don't confuse it with Polysyndeton, Brachylogia. Polysyndeton is its mirror — conjunctions multiplied rather than deleted. Brachylogia is asyndeton between single words.
