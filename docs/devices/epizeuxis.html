Repetition & Refrain

Epizeuxis

eh-pih-ZOOK-sis / ˌɛpɪˈzjuːksɪs

Immediate repetition of a word with nothing between: words, words, words. The bare doubling or tripling registers as overflow — grief, rage, or emphasis that grammar cannot process, only repeat. The least subtle repetition scheme, and therefore the most visceral.

"O horror, horror, horror!" — Macbeth

Don't confuse it with Diacope. Diacope repeats with a word or two intervening ("O villain, villain, smiling, damnèd villain"); epizeuxis allows no gap.
