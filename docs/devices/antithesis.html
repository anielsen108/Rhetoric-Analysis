Balance & Opposition

Antithesis

an-TIH-thuh-sis / ænˈtɪθəsɪs

Setting contrasting ideas in balanced, parallel grammar so the opposition itself becomes visible in the sentence's shape. The symmetry implies the two terms exhaust the possibilities — the world divides cleanly in two — which is why antithesis feels authoritative even when the dichotomy is false.

"It was the best of times, it was the worst of times…" — Dickens

Don't confuse it with Paradox, Oxymoron. Oxymoron compresses contradiction into a two-word phrase; antithesis holds the contrast across balanced clauses. Paradox asserts a contradiction as true rather than balancing alternatives.
