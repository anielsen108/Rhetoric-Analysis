Stichomythia

stik-oh-MITH-ee-uh / ˌstɪkəˈmɪθiə

Dialogue exchanged in rapid, alternating single lines, each speaker capping or reversing the other's words. The tight turn-taking turns conversation into fencing — every line parries the last — and the shared rhythm shows antagonists locked in one mind even as they oppose.

The Hamlet–Gertrude closet scene exchanges.

Don't confuse it with. Ordinary quick dialogue lacks the line-for-line symmetry and echoed vocabulary that make stichomythia a formal duel.
