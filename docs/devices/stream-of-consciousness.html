Stream of Consciousness

Prose that imitates the unedited flow of a mind — associative leaps, unfinished syntax, sensation and memory interleaved without hierarchy. Grammar loosens because the mind being rendered hasn't sorted experience into sentences yet; the reader swims in pre-verbal weather. Joyce, Woolf, and Faulkner made it the modernist signature.

Benjy's section of The Sound and the Fury.

Don't confuse it with Free Indirect Discourse. Free indirect discourse keeps the narrator's grammatical order while borrowing the character's voice; stream of consciousness lets the order itself dissolve.
