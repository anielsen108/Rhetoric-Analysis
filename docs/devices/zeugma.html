Syntax & Word Order

Zeugma

ZOOG-muh / ˈzuːɡmə

One verb (or governing word) yoked to two or more objects that require it in different senses — often one literal, one figurative. The single verb forks midway, and the reader feels the fork as wit: the sentence economizes and jokes in the same motion.

"She arrived in a taxi and a flood of tears."

Don't confuse it with. Syllepsis is often used interchangeably; strictly, syllepsis requires the senses to differ while zeugma is any shared-verb yoking.
