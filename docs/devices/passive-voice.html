Passive Voice

The grammatical subject receives the action rather than performing it — mistakes were made. Passivization deletes or demotes the agent, and that deletion is always information: modesty, evasion, bureaucratic anonymity, or a world where forces act and no one is responsible. As style, it slows and formalizes; as rhetoric, it hides the doer.

"…upon this continent, a new nation, conceived in Liberty" — agency diffused into the passive participles

Don't confuse it with. Not every 'to be' + participle is passive ('he was tired' is adjectival); the test is whether an agent has been displaced.
