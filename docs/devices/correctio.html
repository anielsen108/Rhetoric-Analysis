Correctio / Epanorthosis

kor-REK-tee-oh / eh-pan-or-THOH-sis

Saying something and immediately amending it — 'a hundred, no, a thousand times.' The visible self-correction performs sincerity (the speaker cares enough to be exact) while getting both versions on the record; the first term is never really withdrawn.

"Sixty Million and more" — Morrison's epigraph revising itself upward

Don't confuse it with Aposiopesis. Aposiopesis abandons the sentence; correctio revises it in flight and keeps going.
