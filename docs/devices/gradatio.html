Gradatio / Climax

gruh-DAH-tee-oh / ɡrəˈdɑːtioʊ

A ladder of linked clauses, each picking up the previous one's key word and raising the stakes, until the sequence peaks. The reader climbs step by step, so the summit — the final term — feels earned rather than asserted.

"…tribulation worketh patience; and patience, experience; and experience, hope." — Romans 5:3–4

Don't confuse it with Anadiplosis. One chained repetition is anadiplosis; gradatio is the whole ascending staircase.
