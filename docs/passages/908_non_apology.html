The Non-Apology

A Corporate Press Office · Statement Regarding Recent Events (a composed specimen) · Full text

Thesis of failureThe agentless passive as getaway car, apophasis that mentions what it refuses to discuss, euphemism laid over every noun that could hurt, and a conditional that converts the injured into hypotheses. This is rhetoric's dark mirror — every device competent, every purpose corrupt.

OccasionSomething has gone publicly wrong, and a statement must appear to accept responsibility while accepting none.
PersonaThe first-person plural of no one; contrition's grammar with contrition extracted.

Full dossier

1The Autopsy

Understand this passage and you understand why rhetoric requires an ethics. Nothing here is incompetent — the passive is correctly formed, the apophasis is textbook, the parallelism scans. Every device functions; every device is aimed away from the truth. The tell is distributional: agency appears nowhere near the wrong ("mistakes were made") and promptly reappears for the virtue ("lessons have been learned" — by us, the quick studies). Grammar that misplaces agents this consistently is not confused. It is drafted.

2The Repair

There is only one: "We did it. We were wrong. Here is what changes, and here is the date by which it changes." Active voice, named agent, falsifiable promise. It cannot be written by a committee, which is rather the point.
