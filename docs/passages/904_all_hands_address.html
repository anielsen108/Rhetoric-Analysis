Thesis of failureA pile-up of dead metaphors, reanimated and set loose in the same sentences, where they collide. Each figure was once alive; stacked, they assemble into pictures no one could act on.

OccasionThe quarter was mediocre and the executive must generate urgency without admitting anything specific went wrong.

Full dossier

1The Autopsy

No single phrase here is a crime; every one of them was a serviceable metaphor once, and several still function alone in casual speech. The failure is combinatorial. Metaphor asks the listener to build a picture; this passage asks for six pictures at once, and none of them forms. What the fog is for: not one sentence names a product, a number, a decision, or a date. The mixture is not carelessness. It is camouflage.

2The Repair

Say the thing. "We have three easy wins available this quarter: renew the two lapsed accounts and ship the export feature. If we do that by October, we hit our number." One metaphor ("wins"), kept; everything else converted back into plain nouns a person could act on.
