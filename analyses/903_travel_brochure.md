# Passage 903: The Travel Brochure

**A Regional Tourism Office** · *Discover Coastal Splendour* (a composed specimen) · 2024 | Front panel

**Occasion/Pressure:** A brochure must make one stretch of coastline sound better than the next town's identical stretch. The writer reached for every tool at once.
**Persona/Stance:** Institutional enthusiasm; the voice of no one in particular, delighted about everything.
**Thesis of Effect:** Alliteration run to mania, figures no mind can picture, and clichés laid end to end. Sound has been maximized and sense has quietly left the premises.

---

## PASSAGE

[L1] Nestled in nature's napkin of nourishing niceness, our seaside sanctuary
[L2] simply sizzles with sun-soaked, spirit-soothing splendour.
[L3] Here, memories are made, dreams come true, and every sunset paints a
[L4] picture-perfect postcard of paradise that money simply cannot buy.

---

## A) RHETORICAL TROPES USED

### **Catachresis** (the napkin)
**Lines:** L1
**Definition:** A strained or impossible figure.
**Evidence:** "nature's napkin of nourishing niceness"
**Effect:** No mind can hold this image. Is the coast folded? Is it wiping something? Catachresis at its best produces a productive wrongness — "the watery part of the world" — but this wrongness produces only the question *why a napkin*, and the brochure has no answer.
**Near-miss:** Melville's catachresis defamiliarizes the sea. This one defamiliarizes napkins.

### **Hyperbole** (blanket coverage)
**Lines:** L3–4
**Definition:** Systematic overstatement.
**Evidence:** "every sunset," "paradise that money simply cannot buy"
**Effect:** *Every* sunset. The claim insures itself against no possible evening. Hyperbole persuades when it selects — one sunset, described past believing — and evaporates when it blankets. A paradise that admits every Tuesday is a car park with a view.

---

## B) RHETORICAL SCHEMES USED

### **Alliteration** (mania)
**Lines:** L1–2
**Definition:** Repeated initial sounds in neighbouring words.
**Evidence:** "nestled...nature's napkin of nourishing niceness," "simply sizzles with sun-soaked, spirit-soothing splendour"
**Effect:** Four n-words, then six s-words, chosen for their initials rather than their meanings — "niceness" is in this sentence for no other reason. Alliteration binds two or three words into one gesture; at six, the reader hears a keyboard being leaned on.
**Risk:** Past the third repetition, every alliteration is a limerick waiting to happen.

### **Enumeratio** (of clichés)
**Lines:** L3
**Definition:** A listed accumulation of items.
**Evidence:** "memories are made, dreams come true"
**Effect:** A catalogue whose every item is pre-owned. The list structure promises accumulation — each entry adding something — but clichés add nothing; they are cheques already cashed by other writers. Two of them in series is an inventory of vacancy.

---

## C) MODERN SYNTACTICAL APPROACHES USED

### **Intensifier Dependence**
**Lines:** L2, L4
**Definition:** Adverbs doing work the nouns and verbs have declined.
**Evidence:** "simply sizzles," "simply cannot buy"
**Effect:** "Simply," twice, simplifying nothing. The intensifier is a confession: the writer suspects the verb is weak and hopes an adverb will hold it up. It never does. Prose that trusts its verbs does not send a chaperone.

---

## 1) THE AUTOPSY

Read it aloud and count the decisions made by sound: napkin (for the n), niceness (for the n), sizzles (for the s), splendour (for the s). Nearly half the content words were chosen by their first letters. This is the central corruption — a sentence assembled from the outside in. The clichés then finish the job: where the alliteration was at least energetic, "memories are made" is a phrase that has never made anyone feel anything, which is why committees trust it.

## 2) THE REPAIR

Name things. *"The beach is a mile of white sand. The water is warm until October. At sunset, the whole bay turns copper."* Three facts, one image, no adverbs — and the reader books the ticket, because specificity is the only luxury a brochure can actually deliver.
